# 02 – Presentation & Findings

## Overview

The second phase of the project focused on presenting and communicating the findings from the Tokyo 2020 Olympics statistical analysis.

The goal was to explain the research questions, regression results, and limitations in a clear and understandable way.

## Presentation Activities

### 1. Olympics Analysis Presentation

I prepared a PowerPoint presentation summarising the research questions, analytical approach, regression models, and key findings.

The presentation compared the relationship between athlete count and medal totals with the relationship between discipline count and medal totals.

I used statistical results and visualisations to explain the differences between the two models and communicate the conclusions.

### 2. Interpretation & Discussion

I presented the findings and participated in discussions and peer feedback.

I explained that both variables showed statistically significant positive relationships with medal totals, but athlete count was the stronger predictor.

I also discussed limitations, including outliers, regression assumptions, and the fact that correlation does not establish causation.

## Outcome

This phase helped me develop my ability to **communicate statistical findings, interpret regression results, and explain data-driven conclusions** to an audience.

It also strengthened my understanding of the importance of presenting both the findings and limitations of an analysis.
